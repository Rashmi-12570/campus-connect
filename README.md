Campus Connect

A student-focused campus platform for campus marketplace listings, lost & found, and travel buddy connections.

Features

Campus Marketplace

* List items for sale with price, condition, and reason for selling
* Browse and filter listings based on factors such as price and condition
* Seller dashboard for managing listings
* Buyers can contact sellers directly to discuss purchases and negotiate prices
* Seller ratings and feedback to help build trust between students

Lost & Found

* Post and browse lost or found items within the campus community
* Helps students reconnect with their belongings

Travel Buddy

* Find students travelling to similar destinations
* Connect with others with similar travel plans, such as trips to the airport

Tech Stack

* React
* TypeScript
* Vite
* Tailwind CSS
* Supabase
* Lucide React
* ESLint

Live Demo

Project Purpose

Campus Connect brings useful student-focused services into one platform, making it easier to exchange items, find lost belongings, and connect with students who have similar travel plans.
